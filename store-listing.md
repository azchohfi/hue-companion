# Microsoft Store Listing — Hue Companion

## Description

Hue Companion is a native Windows app for controlling your Philips Hue smart lights. Built with WinUI 3 — the same framework behind Windows 11 system apps — it launches fast, uses minimal memory, and feels right at home on your desktop.

DASHBOARD
See all your rooms and zones at a glance. Each room shows its current lighting state with live color indicators. Toggle lights on and off, adjust brightness, or tap into a room for full control — all from a clean, card-based dashboard.

ROOM & LIGHT CONTROL
Dive into any room to control individual lights. Set exact colors with a full color picker, adjust color temperature from warm to cool, and fine-tune brightness with responsive sliders. Every change is reflected on your real lights instantly.

SCENE LIBRARY
Browse and activate your Hue scenes with one click. Scene cards show color palettes so you can see what you're getting before you activate. Works with both room scenes and zone scenes across all your bridges.

SCENE BUILDER — A BUILT-IN ANIMATION STUDIO
Create your own animated lighting scenes with a DAW-style timeline editor. Place keyframes on a multi-track timeline to define exact colors and brightness at any point in time. Add event triggers like lightning flashes, sparkles, and candle flickers for dynamic, randomized effects. Preview your creation on your real lights as you build it, then save and replay anytime.

NATIVE HUE EFFECTS
Access 10 built-in Hue effects powered by the Hue bridge's native engine: Fire, Candle, Sparkle, Glisten, Opal, Prism, Underwater, Cosmos, Sunbeam, and Enchant. Adjust speed and brightness, then apply to any light.

GLOBAL HOTKEYS
Map keyboard shortcuts to your favorite scenes and actions. Control your lights from anywhere on your desktop — even when the app is minimized or hidden in the system tray. Your lights, your layout.

SYSTEM TRAY
Minimize Hue Companion to the system tray so it's always one click away without cluttering your taskbar. Optionally launch at Windows startup so your light controls are ready the moment you sit down.

MULTI-BRIDGE SUPPORT
Connect multiple Hue Bridges simultaneously. All your rooms, zones, and lights from every bridge appear together in one unified interface.

AI INTEGRATION (MCP SERVER)
Hue Companion includes a built-in Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that lets AI assistants control your lights using natural language. Connect to Claude Desktop, Claude Code, VS Code Copilot, and other MCP-compatible tools. Tell your AI to "set the living room to warm white at 50%" or "create a sunset animation" and Hue Companion handles the rest. Setup takes just a few clicks from the Settings page.

DESIGNED FOR WINDOWS
Every pixel is crafted to look and feel native on Windows 10 and Windows 11. Full support for dark and light themes. Smooth animations throughout the interface. Fast startup (under a second) and low memory usage (around 42 MB).

REQUIREMENTS
- One or more Philips Hue Bridges on your local network
- Philips Hue lights connected to your bridge(s)
- Windows 10 version 1809 or later

Hue Companion is not affiliated with Philips Hue or Signify.
